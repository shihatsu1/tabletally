# Code of conduct

TableTally is made for friends around a table, and we want working on it to feel the same way.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or to these card games.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people. No insults, harassment, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Respect privacy: do not post anyone's personal details, and keep real names out of fixtures and screenshots.
- House rules differ from table to table and region to region. Treat another group's rules as a variation, not a mistake.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainers through the contact on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and may ban people who do not follow this code.
